🔐 QKYB - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 21 Oct 2026
App: QKYB - Quantum UP Wallet | https://qkyb-wallet.vercel.app

1. No Data Collection - 100% Non-Custodial

QKYB does NOT collect, store or share your private key, 12-word seed phrase, PIN or transaction data on any server. Everything is stored only on your device (localStorage). We never see your seed.

2. Blockchain Use

Real POL balance check uses public RPC: https://rpc-amoy.polygon.technology and explorer https://amoy.polygonscan.com — This is public blockchain data, not private.

3. No Tracking

No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your address is public but not linked to your name.

4. Local Storage

We save only: wallet address (public), name you enter, UP-BR code. Clear anytime via
